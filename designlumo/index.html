What it does

You describe the design you want, pick a format like a social post, banner or carousel, and DesignLumo generates a few styles to choose from. Every result opens in a full editor, so you can change the text, colors and layout instead of regenerating from scratch.

  • Brand kits keep your colors and assets consistent across every design.
  • Chat-based generation turns a one-line prompt into finished, editable layouts.
  • Export to PNG, JPG, WebP, SVG or PDF.
