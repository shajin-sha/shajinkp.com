How I bootstrapped DesignLumo to an exit, part-time with $0 on ads

Publish date · About 4 min read

DesignLumo was my first real product, and my first exit. I built it alone, from scratch, in the hours around a full-time job. It never had a marketing budget. Every customer came from organic marketing, and it still grew to around $400 in monthly recurring revenue and $1.5K+ in total revenue before it was acquired.

This is how that happened, and what I'd tell anyone trying the same thing.

Why I built it

When did you start, and what problem made you want to build an AI design tool? Who was it for?

Building it part-time

I had a full-time job the whole way through, so DesignLumo got evenings and weekends. That forced me to keep it small: one person, one stack (React, Express, and Fabric.js for the editor, with Gemini and the OpenAI API doing the generation), and only the features that mattered.

The core idea was simple: describe a design, get a few styles back, and then actually edit the one you like. Most AI image tools give you a flat picture. DesignLumo gives you a real layout with text, colors and elements you can change, plus brand kits so everything stays on-brand.

The first customer was a stranger in Ireland

My first paying customer wasn't a friend or someone from my network. It was a complete stranger from Ireland. That moment mattered more than any revenue number since: someone I'd never met found the product, trusted it, and paid for it.

How did they find DesignLumo? Do you remember what they were designing?

Growing with $0 on ads

I didn't spend anything on marketing. All of DesignLumo's growth came from organic channels.
